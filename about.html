“ The greatest high is an expanding mind.”

My work lives in the space of pure abstraction. Driven equally by instinct and thought. Rooted in non-representational art and the subconscious, my paintings evolve organically through gestural expression and spontaneity.

Until recently (2026), I worked without any external references, letting each piece emerge through intuition and an ongoing dialogue with the canvas. While I am now exploring ways to make the work more accessible to the eye, yet my strongest and most joyful pieces are still those born from pure imagination.

Words have been part of my expression since my earliest canvases around 2011. Like many artists on this path, I come from a self-directed background, outside formal art education. This independence allows intuition to lead over convention.

“Believing in myself like no one else, yet painting for everyone else.”
— My name is Martin Hanispak — thank you so much for stopping by.
